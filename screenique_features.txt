Features of Screenique:

1. **Authentication & User Management**
   - Secure Login and Signup via Firebase Authentication
   - Google Sign-In integration
   - Customizable User Profiles (Display Name, Unique Username, Bio, Profile Picture)

2. **Movie & TV Show Discovery**
   - Powered by TMDB API for extensive, up-to-date movie/series data
   - Home Screen Recommendation Carousels for trending and curated movies
   - Theatre Carousel for highlighting new releases
   - Detailed Movie/Series Information Screens (Posters, Synopsis, Release Date, Cast, Crew, Trailer links)
   - Upcoming movies and series section

3. **Personal Collections & Tracking**
   - Watchlist: Save movies and TV shows to watch later
   - Watched List: Log movies and TV shows you have already seen
   - Rating System: Rate your watched content out of 5 stars
   - Profile Statistics: Track your total watched count with a breakdown of movies vs. series

4. **Community & Social Interaction**
   - User Profiles and Public Profiles: View other people's cinematic journey
   - Follower/Following System: Follow other cinephiles and build a network
   - User Search: Find your friends or popular users on the platform
   - Archive Rank System: Earn unique titles and ranks based on the total number of movies you've watched
   - Top 5 Spotlight: Pin your top 5 favorite movies to your profile

5. **Cinecast (Broadcast Wire)**
   - Social Feed: Broadcast thoughts, reviews, and movie recommendations to the community feed
   - Engagement: Like and comment on other users' broadcasts
   - Mentions and direct engagement on posts

6. **World Cinema Explorer**
   - Interactive World Map highlighting cinematic output from different countries
   - Country Dossier Sheets detailing top movies, trends, and iconic directors from specific regions

7. **Experiences Hub**
   - "Best Experience Hub": Document and share your most memorable theatrical or viewing experiences

8. **Entertainment News**
   - Dedicated "The News" tab for live transmissions and the latest entertainment news updates

9. **Director & Cast Profiles**
   - Explore detailed profiles of film directors and actors
   - Browse a director's complete filmography

10. **Sharing & Notifications**
    - Instagram Share Integration: Beautifully format and share your watched movies or recommendations to IG stories
    - Real-time Notifications: Stay updated on Likes, Follows, Comments, and new Cinecasts

11. **Search & History**
    - Advanced global search for movies, series, and people
    - Search History: Keep track of and easily revisit "Recent Investigations"
    - Quick-Add functionality: Add to Watchlist or Watched list directly from the search results

12. **Settings & Customization**
    - Account Management: Edit Bio, Display Name, and Profile Privacy settings
    - Custom Design System: Unique Brutalist-inspired UI mixed with modern aesthetics
    - Reset Cinema Journey: Option to clear data and start fresh
    - Secure Logout
